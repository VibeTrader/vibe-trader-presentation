# Slide 2: evidence for trader pain points

Research checked September 9, 2026. Heading: **Why trading decisions are hard**.

The family story is founder-provided motivation. External research supports broader trader problems; it does not establish what VibeTrader's own users experience or validate the product's effectiveness.

## Strategy performance changes

Daniel and Moskowitz's *Momentum Crashes* finds that momentum strategies can experience severe losses in particular market states, especially rebounds following market declines and high volatility. This supports condition-dependent strategy performance, not a claim that every strategy inevitably stops working.

Suggested wording: **Strategies can lose their edge as markets change.**

Source: https://www.nber.org/papers/w20439

## Understanding current conditions

The same paper illustrates why a strategy's average historical performance does not describe every market state. It does not directly establish that users have “no clarity.” That is a customer-research claim requiring interviews, surveys, or product evidence.

Suggested wording: **It is hard to judge when a strategy fits current conditions.** Treat this as a user pain point to validate, rather than a measured finding from the paper.

## Behavioral decision-making

Grinblatt and Keloharju combine equity trading data with tax records, driving records, and psychological profiles and find that sensation seeking and overconfidence are associated with trading activity. This supports a narrower behavioral-bias claim; it does not directly measure every form of emotional or inconsistent decision-making among retail FX/CFD users.

Suggested wording: **Overconfidence and emotion can undermine trading discipline.** The overconfidence component has direct support here; the broader emotion and discipline language is an interpretation.

Source: https://www.nber.org/papers/w12223

## Platform incentives and decision support

The FCA's 2024 experiment with more than 9,000 consumers found that some digital engagement practices, including push notifications and prize draws, can increase trading frequency and risk taking. This supports a claim about specific app features, not a universal claim about all platforms or their intentions.

Evidence-backed alternative: **Some trading apps encourage more trading and greater risk-taking.**

Source: https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/fca-keeps-trading-apps-under-review-over-gaming-concerns

The existing statement “Existing platforms optimize execution, not decisions” is too broad. MetaTrader 5 explicitly offers technical and fundamental analysis plus strategy testing and optimization. A narrower differentiation claim about missing personalized, ongoing decision support would require a competitor feature review and customer evidence.

Sources:
- https://www.metatrader5.com/en/trading-platform
- https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help/algotrading/testing
